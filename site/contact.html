What we will need

To confirm fit, send us a short note.

Email is fine — we don't require a formal RFI. A few lines on the pursuit lets us route to the right Partner and confirm capacity within 48 hours.

Tell us

  • Project name, location, sector, scale
  • Delivery model (IPD / CMAR / P3 / PDB / traditional)
  • Start date and key milestones
  • Prime or contractor leading the pursuit, if known
  • Specific MCW personnel or experience requested

You'll hear back

24 hrs: acknowledgement · 48 hrs: proposed VEC team · 5 business days: tailored capability statement.
